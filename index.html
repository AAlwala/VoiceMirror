How the feedback works (please read once)
  • Speech-to-text: Chrome's built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text. Chrome usually sends the audio to Google's servers to do this, so it needs an internet connection.
  • All feedback here is rule-based, not AI. VoiceMirror checks the transcript with transparent local rules: word matching, filler-word lists, repetition counts, and a list of common grammar patterns. It can miss mistakes and it cannot understand meaning the way a teacher or an AI can.
  • What it does not measure: pronunciation accuracy, accent, intonation, confidence, or emotion. A word that wasn't recognised might be a recognition error, not your mistake. Your accent is never scored.
  • Your privacy: progress is saved only in this browser (localStorage). Recordings stay in memory until you leave the page, unless you choose to download them.
